Hi, I'm Kartik. I'm a machine learning engineer and researcher, currently working at Nextdoor where I build the models that power the app's content feed.

I always enjoy chatting with other people in the ML space. If you want to talk about new ideas, collaborate on a project, or just share notes on what you're working on, feel free to reach out.
